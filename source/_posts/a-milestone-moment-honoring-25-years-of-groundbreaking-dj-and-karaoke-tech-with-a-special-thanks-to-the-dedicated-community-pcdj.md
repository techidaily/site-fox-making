## A Milestone Moment: Honoring 25 Years of Groundbreaking DJ & Karaoke Tech with a Special Thanks to the Dedicated Community – PCDJ

[![PCDJ Celebrating 25 Years in Business](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/celebrating25yers.jpg?resize=845%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/celebrating25yers.jpg?fit=1030%2C579&ssl=1 "celebrating25yers")

Hey there, PCDJ’s!

It’s a momentous occasion here at PCDJ.com as we hit a remarkable milestone – our **25th Year Anniversary**! That’s right, for a quarter of a century, we’ve been at the forefront of DJ and karaoke software technology, and we couldn’t have done it without you, our loyal customers.

![Celebrating 25 Years at PCDJ](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Gold-Anniversary-Badge-Round-Logo-copy.png?resize=201%2C201&ssl=1)

Way back in 1999, we embarked on a journey to revolutionize the DJ and karaoke scene. And look at us now! We’re proud to say that we were the first to bring professional DJ software with headphone cueing into the limelight. But that’s not all – in collaboration with Numark, we co-designed the first commercially sold DJ controller, the DMC-1, a true game-changer in the industry.

Fast forward to today, and we’re thrilled to offer DEX 3, our most robust and comprehensive DJ software solution yet. It’s been a fantastic journey of continuous innovation, and we’re not stopping anytime soon. There’s a lot on our roadmap for this year and beyond, and we’re excited to bring you along for the ride.

One thing that sets us apart in this ever-changing world is our commitment to you – our policy of free updates for both our DJ and karaoke software solutions. We believe in providing value and continuous improvements to our users, unlike the standard practice in the software publishing realm today.

We want to extend a massive thank you to each and every one of you who has been part of our journey. Whether you’ve been with us since the beginning or just joined the PCDJ family, your support means everything. Here’s to another 25 years of innovation, music, and fun!

With gratitude and excitement, 

**Ryan Sherr**

PCDJ General Manager
