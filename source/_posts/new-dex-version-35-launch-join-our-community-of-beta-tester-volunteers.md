## New DEX Version 3.5 Launch: Join Our Community of Beta Tester Volunteers!

[![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/dex35betatest-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/dex35betatest-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "dex35betatest-coverimage")

DEX 3 is Digital 1 Audio’s flagship **[DJ program](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)** for mixing audio and music videos, and[version 3.4 dropped just a couple weeks ago](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/). Our mad scientists are already working diligently on “_what’s next” –_ and we need your help…

**DEX 3.5** will introduce a [new and improved GUI](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)(_default skin_) for better access and usability of many of the new features DEX 3.4 provides, notably the new sample deck functions which support many of the playback controls found in the full-fledged DEX 3 decks.

[FIRST LOOK AT DEX 3.5 SKIN ![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/11863478_10153133194937615_6809496170181639480_n.jpg?fit=300%2C300&ssl=1 "11863478_10153133194937615_6809496170181639480_n")](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

While we can’t let the proverbial cat out of the bag publicly yet, that’s not why we’re petitioning for a small exclusive group of 3.5 BETA testers.

We feel for many the DEX 3.5 update will be a game-changer (_a term thrown around too often and too lightly these days, but appropriate — we feel — in this instance_) and we want your help to ensure it’s rock-solid and road-ready.

If you meet the BETA Tester requirements below please fill out the form at the bottom of this post and someone from our production team will get back to you soon.

### DEX 3.5 Beta Team Requirements

* A current owner of PCDJ DEX 3
* You must own a computer that meets the minimum DEX 3 system requirements:

#### System Requirements

**WINDOWS**

Windows: 7 / Windows 8 / Windows 8.1 / Windows 10  
2.5GHz Intel Core 2 Duo / Core 2 Quad / Core i3/i5/i7 / Amd Phenon (Celeron Not Recommended)  
4 GIG RAM or Better  
Video Card: Dedicated Graphics Card with at least 512MB (or Intel HD 3000 Series or better)  
DirectX/ASIO Compatible Multi-Channel Audio Interface  
200 MB free on the hard-drive

**MAC**

Mac: OSX 10.7.5 (Lion) or greater  
2.5GHz Intel Core 2 Duo / Core i3/i5/i7  
4 GIG RAM or Better  
Video Card: Dedicated Graphics Card with at least 512MB (or Intel HD 3000 Series or better)  
Multichannel Core Audio soundcard  
200 MB free on the hard-drive

* You must be willing to sign a simple NDA ([Non-Disclosure Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-disclosure%5Fagreement)) — and therefore have the ability to sign, scan and email back a copy.
* You will be required to report on any issues and/or usability concerns. Anyone that doesn’t actively participate in reporting will be removed from the beta team.

#### Bonus Preferance

* Have one of the 75+ DEX 3 supported [DJ controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

If you meet these requirements please submit the form below. A PCDJ production team member will get back to you. (**NOTE:** _Not all testers will be accepted as we have limited space on the BETA testing team_)
