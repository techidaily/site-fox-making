While we _get it_, it’s important to note that quite a few bars and restaurants have relied on internet/cable type connections to stream audio in their venues for years. More importantly, as technology lunges forward every day leaders in the sector such as SpaceX will soon make “[Reliable WiFi for all](https://qz.com/1618386/spacex-launches-first-starlink-internet-satellites/)” a reality. Think 10,000+ satellites in orbit with redundancy offering every person on earth a truly mind-blowingly-fast connection — no matter the location they’re in.
