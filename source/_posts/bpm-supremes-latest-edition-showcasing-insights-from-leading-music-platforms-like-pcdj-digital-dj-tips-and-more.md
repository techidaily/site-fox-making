Released today in version 1.2 of the BPM Supreme mobile app, BPM News allows users to read all of their favorite DJ and music industry news in one place.

**San Diego, CA, October 29, 2019** – BPM Supreme, a leading music delivery service for professional DJs, has launched BPM News, a news feed curation inside of the BPM Supreme mobile app featuring content from trusted partner news channels like DJ Times, Digital DJ Tips, Ableton, Mobile Beat, Morgan Page (MP) Quick Tips, and PCDJ.

“BPM News is a fresh way to experience and read the latest DJ-focused news and culture,” said Angel “AROCK” Castillo, CEO and founder of BPM Supreme. “We strive to continuously evolve our product so it is the most useful tool a DJ can have, and this new feature helps us further meet that goal.”

In version 1.2 of the BPM Supreme mobile app available now, users will find thousands of articles alongside BPM Supreme’s extensive music library. With completely optimized formatting, BPM News displays articles in a crisp, easy-to-read view that keeps all photos, videos, and links intact. Users are able to click through to read an article directly on a publication’s website, follow their favorite channels, and more.

“This curated news feed allows our members to discover news and music simultaneously by browsing articles, listening to tracks, and adding to their crate on the go,” added AROCK.

BPM News is a free feature on the BPM Supreme mobile app, available on the [App Store](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/bpm-supreme/id1435624754?mt=8) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bpmmobile). More news channels will be added in late 2019, including Pioneer DJ and HipHopDX. Learn more about the BPM Supreme mobile app at [bpmsupreme.com/app](https://www.bpmsupreme.com/app) and [more about memberships at bpmsupreme.com](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)
