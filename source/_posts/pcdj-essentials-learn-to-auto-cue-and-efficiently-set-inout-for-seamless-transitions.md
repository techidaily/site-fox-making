### WHAT IS AUTOCUE?

**Autocue** is enabled by default (_autocue can be disabled in options_) in [DEX 3.4](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and will automatically trim the silence (_if present_) from the start and ends of all your audio, music video and karaoke files.

This means when you load a track to one of the four available decks in DEX 3 and click play, **your music will start instantly with zero “_dead air_“.** DEX 3.4 displays your autocue points along the track timeline as red “_flags_” labeled “_in_” and “_out_“. You can reset these cue points by simply right-clicking on the “_in_” or “_out_” buttons below each deck, and then reset them as you see fit.

The wonderful thing about the revamped **autocue feature** is that the autocue points will be used as mix points for automix.   **Trim the long intro or outtro** using the new autocue mix points for more **seamless transitions** between tracks when using automix.
